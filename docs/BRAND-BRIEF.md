# HL SUPPLY
### Bespoke Product Design & Development for Luxury Hospitality

## Who We Are

HL Supply is a bespoke product design, development, and supply company specializing in luxury hospitality. We partner with some of the world's most distinguished hotels, resorts, and independent boutique properties to create thoughtfully designed merchandise, guest amenities, and signature accessories that reflect the unique character of each destination.

Our clientele consists primarily of five-star resorts, internationally recognized luxury hotel groups, independent luxury hotels, and boutique hospitality properties where presentation, individuality, and attention to detail are fundamental to the guest experience.

We operate at the intersection of **product design, luxury hospitality, and custom manufacturing**, helping properties translate their identities into beautiful, functional, and distinctive physical products.

## Our Philosophy

We believe that every exceptional hotel has its own identity—and the products associated with that property should reflect it.

Luxury hospitality is defined not only by architecture, interiors, and service, but also by the smaller details guests interact with throughout their stay. From a beautifully designed beach tote to a carefully considered in-room accessory, these objects become part of the property's overall experience.

We believe such products should feel intentional, not generic. They should complement their surroundings, serve a genuine purpose, and reflect the standards of the hotel they represent.

Our philosophy goes beyond traditional branded merchandise. **We don't simply believe in placing a hotel's logo on a product. We believe in creating products that feel inherently connected to the hotel itself.**

Sometimes that means a bold, recognizable signature piece. Other times, it means understated colors, refined materials, subtle branding, and thoughtful construction.

The objective is always the same: to create something that feels authentic to the property's identity.

## What We Do

HL Supply provides an extensive range of customizable merchandise, guest amenities, and hospitality accessories.

Our core collections include premium tote bags and resort bags, hats and headwear, reusable bottles, resort footwear, hotel and spa slippers, leather goods, in-room accessories, sunglasses, umbrellas, wellness products, and other guest essentials.

Our products serve a wide range of purposes throughout a property.

For hotel boutiques and retail collections, we help develop distinctive merchandise that guests can purchase, enjoy during their stay, and take home as a lasting connection to the destination.

For guestrooms and suites, we provide customizable accessories and amenities that complement the property's interior design and guest experience.

For spas, pools, beach clubs, wellness facilities, golf resorts, VIP programs, and special events, we help create products that combine practical function with the property's individual aesthetic.

Whether intended for retail, everyday guest use, or a special experience, every product presents an opportunity to express the hotel's identity.

## Our Greatest Strength: Bespoke Design & Creative Freedom

One of HL Supply's defining characteristics is our extensive product customization and development capability.

**Our catalog is not a limitation. It is a starting point for inspiration.**

Unlike traditional merchandise suppliers that primarily offer standardized products with added logos, HL Supply has a full design team capable of working with clients to substantially modify existing products or develop entirely original creations.

A hotel may discover a bag in our collection that it likes but envision something completely different. It may want to change the dimensions, shape, materials, construction, handles, interior details, colors, or overall aesthetic.

We can work with the client to explore those changes and develop a product around its particular vision.

Our capabilities extend beyond adapting existing designs.

If a property has an original concept, a sketch, a reference image, or simply an idea for something it would like to create, our team can collaborate with it to explore bringing that product to life from the ground up.

This approach gives our clients considerable creative freedom.

Rather than being restricted to what already exists, they can pursue products developed specifically around their property's identity, functional requirements, and guest expectations.

All concepts are evaluated with the practical considerations of materials, production feasibility, quantities, and commercial requirements in mind.

## Our Approach to Luxury

We understand that luxury is not a single aesthetic.

A historic European estate, a contemporary urban hotel, a secluded tropical resort, and an intimate mountain retreat may each have entirely different interpretations of what luxury should look and feel like.

We do not believe in imposing one design style across every client.

Our role is to understand the property's existing identity and work within that creative direction.

For some hotels, that might mean traditional craftsmanship, natural materials, and heritage-inspired details.

For others, it may mean contemporary silhouettes, minimal branding, refined textures, and architectural simplicity.

We appreciate that true sophistication often comes from restraint. A product does not need an oversized logo to feel unmistakably associated with a particular property.

The materials, proportions, colors, functionality, and smallest design details can collectively express its identity.

## More Than a Product Supplier

HL Supply aims to build relationships based on creative collaboration, understanding, and trust.

We want to understand not just which product a hotel is interested in, but why that product matters, how it will be used, who will interact with it, and what it should communicate about the property.

A boutique director may be seeking a distinctive collection that guests genuinely want to own.

A director of rooms may be looking for guest accessories that feel consistent with a newly renovated suite.

A spa director may envision a coordinated set of wellness products designed specifically for the facility.

A hotel owner may have an entirely original idea for a signature item that does not yet exist.

We approach each of these opportunities with the same philosophy: **understand the vision first, then explore the product that best brings it to life.**

Our role extends from supplying established designs to collaborating on entirely new products, combining creative flexibility with the practical considerations required to turn concepts into tangible goods.
