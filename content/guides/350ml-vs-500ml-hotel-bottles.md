# 350 ml vs 500 ml: Choosing the Right Custom Bottle Size for Hotels and Spas

Size is the first question on a bottle brief and the one that most often gets answered by preference rather than program. The right answer depends on where the bottle lives: a nightstand, a treatment lounge, a gym floor, a hiking trail, or a boutique shelf. This comparison lays out the two sizes available on every HL Supply bottle, what each is for, and how the four styles behave in each.

## At a glance

| | 350 ml | 500 ml |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capacity | About one serving; the standard suite and spa pour | A refill-and-go bottle for all-day use |
| Weight when full | Lighter on a nightstand and in a spa robe pocket | Heavier, but fewer refills on the gym floor or a trail |
| Presentation | Slim, carafe-like beside a filtered-water station | Substantial, reads as equipment |
| Typical programs | Suites, turndown, spa, boutique retail | Fitness, outdoor, pool deck, conferences, events |
| Available on | All four styles | All four styles |

## The case for 350 ml

A suite bottle is picked up, poured, and put back. At 350 ml it holds roughly one serving, sits comfortably on a nightstand or a bathroom ledge, and matches the scale of the glassware around it. In a spa it slips into a robe pocket or rests beside a treatment bed without crowding the tray. On a retail shelf it displays more bottles per foot and reads as an object rather than equipment. The Aura, with its flush-mount lid and seamless lines, is the most carafe-like of the four at this size, which is why it is the usual suite choice.

## The case for 500 ml

A bottle that goes to the gym, on a guided hike, or to the beach club for an afternoon needs to carry enough to avoid constant refills. At 500 ml it is the working size: substantial in hand, visibly insulated, and able to see a guest through a class or a walk. The Vanguard's stainless swing handle and rugged body suit this scale, and the Marquis's integrated carry loop makes the larger size easy to clip and carry. For conferences and retreats, 500 ml is the size that sits on a table through a session.

## Insulation at both sizes

All four bottles are vacuum-insulated stainless steel at either size, with triple-wall construction specified on the Marquis and the Aura. Insulation performance scales with the bottle, so both sizes keep water cold and coffee hot for hours; a larger volume simply holds its temperature a little longer because there is more of it. The lid and body construction are the same at both sizes, so the choice is purely about capacity and presence.

## Logo and color at each size

Both sizes carry a laser-etched logo in any custom body color. The practical difference is scale: a wordmark that fits comfortably on a 500 ml body may crowd a 350 ml one, and a crest sized for the smaller bottle can look timid on the larger. When a property runs both sizes, the mockup should show the etch on each, and the proof should confirm placement and scale for each size separately. Body color is chosen once and matched across both.

## Deciding by program

1. **Suites and turndown:** 350 ml, usually the Aura or the Marquis.
2. **Spa and wellness lounges:** 350 ml, the Marquis.
3. **Fitness, guided activities, outdoor:** 500 ml, the Vanguard or the Marquis.
4. **Boutique retail:** 350 ml displays better; carry 500 ml of the same style if the property has an active program.
5. **Conferences and retreats:** 500 ml, the Aura or the Lumière.
6. **Travel gift:** 350 ml Lumière.

Properties running more than one program usually specify one style in both sizes rather than two styles, so the identity is consistent from the suite to the trail and both sizes can be quoted on one project.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which bottle size is best for hotel suites?

350 ml. It fits a nightstand and matches the scale of the glassware, and the Aura's flush-mount lid and seamless lines make it the usual suite carafe.

### Which size is best for fitness and outdoor programs?

500 ml. It carries enough for a class or a hike without constant refills; the Vanguard's swing handle and the Marquis's carry loop suit the larger bottle.

### Can we order both sizes of the same style?

Yes. Every style is available in 350 ml and 500 ml, and specifying one style in both sizes keeps the identity consistent across programs. Each size is a separate line in the quote.

### Is the insulation the same at both sizes?

Yes. All four bottles are vacuum-insulated stainless steel at either size, with triple-wall construction on the Marquis and the Aura, keeping drinks cold or hot for hours.

### Does the logo change between sizes?

The method (laser etching) and the artwork do not change, but scale and placement are confirmed separately for each size on the proof so the mark sits correctly on both bodies.
