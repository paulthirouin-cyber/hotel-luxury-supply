# Custom Hotel Guest Accessories: The Complete Buyer's Guide

Accessories are the category where a property expresses care in small, specific ways: a sleep mask on the pillow, a lens cloth beside the sunglasses, an umbrella in the wardrobe before a coastal shower, a yoga mat on the terrace for the morning session. Individually they are modest; as a considered set they are the difference between a room and a stay. This guide covers the seven pieces in the HL Supply collection, the logo methods, how to combine them, and the quantities behind each.

## Where accessories work on a property

- **Suites and turndown.** Sleep mask, lens cloth, and cable organization as in-room amenities.
- **Pool deck and beach club.** Sunglasses for retail, umbrellas for sudden weather, and a lens cloth to keep lenses clean.
- **Spa and wellness programs.** Yoga mats for in-room practice, terrace sessions, and the studio.
- **Arrival and VIP gifts.** A sleep mask and lens cloth in a mini tote; sunglasses for a VIP arrival.
- **Retail.** Sunglasses and umbrellas as boutique items with real margin.

## Logo methods

Laser etching is the method across the collection except the sleep mask: the logo is engraved into acetate, wood, microfiber, or mat material without ink, so it is permanent and does not wear off. The Solace is embroidered, because thread suits a plush textile and feels right against the face. Supply vector artwork; the mockup shows the mark at temple, handle, cloth, mask, and mat scale before the proof is approved. Placement is confirmed on the proof.

## Color and coordination

Every piece is produced in any custom color, and the strongest programs coordinate rather than match. ## Writing the brief

An accessories brief works best organized by set rather than by item: which pieces go in the suite set, the pool set, and the wellness set, in which colors, with which logo treatment, for which room count or program size. Add the logo in vector format, Pantone references for each color, the quantity per piece, and the date the first delivery is needed. If pieces are gifts, say where they are presented (the valet tray, the pillow, the mini tote at arrival) so packaging can be specified to match; if they are retail, add how they will be displayed.

Because minimums differ by item, the quote is where a set is balanced. The yoga mat reaches its minimum at 100 units, the umbrella at 200, and the sunglasses, lens cloth, and sleep mask at 250. A suite set for a 120-key property might pair 250 sleep masks and 250 lens cloths with 100 yoga mats for the studio, and the concierge can show the tiers side by side before anything is confirmed.

## Common mistakes, and how to avoid them

- **Buying items instead of sets.** A sleep mask in one color and a lens cloth in another, ordered months apart, do not read as a collection. Specify the set in one quote.
- **Choosing sunglasses by fashion alone.** Polarized lenses matter beside water and sand; acetate matters for comfort and color. Match the frame to where it will be worn and sold.
- **A full-size logo on a temple arm.** Etch scale on acetate is small. Use a wordmark or a simple icon and approve it at true scale on the mockup.
- **Skipping the umbrella.** It is the amenity guests are most grateful for on the one afternoon it rains, and the laser-etched handle keeps the canopy quiet.
- **Over-ordering the mat, under-ordering the mask.** Yoga mats are studio stock; sleep masks and lens cloths leave with guests. Plan replacement rhythms separately.
