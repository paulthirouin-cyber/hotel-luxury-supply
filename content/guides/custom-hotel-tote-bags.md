# Custom Hotel Tote Bags: The Complete Buyer's Guide

The tote is the most-photographed object a resort will ever put its name on. It leaves the property every morning, sits beside a lounger all afternoon, rides a yacht transfer, and goes home in a suitcase. For that reason it deserves more thought than a logo on a stock bag. This guide walks through the decisions a hotel, resort, private club, or beach club makes when commissioning custom tote bags, with the facts from the HL Supply catalog where they apply.

## Who buys custom hotel totes, and why

Three programs account for almost every tote project.

- **Retail collections.** The boutique or pro shop sells a tote guests genuinely want. These bags carry the most design ambition: woven leather, contrast stripes, leather straps, structured shapes.
- **Guest amenities.** The tote waits in the room at arrival or is handed over at the pool desk. Here durability, a clean silhouette, and an easy fold matter more than novelty.
- **Events and VIP programs.** Golf tournaments, destination weddings, owners' weekends, and openings use a tote as the carrier for everything else in the welcome kit.

The same style can serve two programs at once. A property often runs a canvas amenity tote and a premium woven retail tote in the same colorway, so the guest sees one identity whether the bag was gifted or bought.

## Choosing the material

**Cotton canvas** is the default for a reason. It is durable, natural in hand, takes custom dyeing and embroidery cleanly, and reads as resort rather than promotional. Within canvas there are still choices: the Signature's classic weight for everyday use, the Pavilion's heavy-duty structured canvas that stands on its own, and the Nomad's softer, breathable canvas that cinches closed for a spa or gym bag.

**Hand-woven vegan leather** moves the tote into the boutique tier. The Atelier is the largest bag in the collection at 20 inches wide, with a hand-crafted weave that photographs beautifully and holds a deep custom color. Properties use it as the anchor of a retail collection, priced and merchandised as a fashion object.

**Leather trim on canvas** is the middle path. The Sovereign pairs a cotton canvas body with genuine leather straps, so the bag gains the weight and patina of leather handles without a full leather price. The Envoy achieves a similar lift with contrast accent straps in a second brand color.

**Stripes and color blocking** suit coastal properties. The Nautical's contrast stripes can match an umbrella line, a cabana awning, or a pool towel, and the style is offered in any custom color pairing rather than a stock stripe.

## Sizing: match the bag to the moment

Dimensions in the catalog are width by height. A few rules of thumb from how properties actually use them:

- **Pool and beach (15–16″ wide, 14–18″ tall):** room for a towel, a book, sunscreen, and a bottle. The Signature, Nautical, and Sovereign all sit here. Taller bags like the Nautical carry a rolled towel upright.
- **Shopping and city (16–18″ wide):** the Pavilion's squared base keeps purchases flat; the Envoy's clean lines suit an urban boutique.
- **Statement and travel (20″ wide):** the Atelier doubles as a weekend carryall.
- **Gift and VIP (10″ wide):** the Petite is the right scale for an arrival gift that holds a bottle, a lens cloth, and a note card without looking empty.
- **Wellness (14″ wide, drawstring):** the Nomad carries slippers, a yoga mat strap, and a bottle to the spa.

If the exact dimensions matter to a program (a specific towel, a retail shelf, a packaging box), the Bespoke Atelier can adjust size, pockets, strap length, and closures starting from any catalog style.

## Logo methods and placement

Embroidery is the finish across the entire tote collection. It is the premium choice for canvas because the mark is stitched into the fabric rather than printed on it: it does not crack, fade, or peel, and it has a dimensional quality guests associate with quality luggage. Three practical points:

1. **Supply vector artwork.** AI, EPS, SVG, or a vector PDF lets the logo be digitized accurately. Fine serifs and tiny taglines are simplified for stitch legibility, which the mockup will show.
2. **Choose thread color deliberately.** A tone-on-tone crest in a thread one shade off the canvas reads as luxury; a contrast crest reads as retail. Both are correct, for different programs.
3. **Placement is confirmed on the proof.** Centered on the front panel is standard; offset or near-handle placements suit smaller marks and striped bodies.

For a different logo treatment, such as a woven label, a leather patch, or a printed interior, start a bespoke request from the product page and the atelier will advise what is feasible at your quantity.

## Timelines

Plan on **4 to 6 weeks of production after proof approval**, plus transit to the property. The proof is the gate: artwork delays are the usual reason a tote program misses an opening. A realistic schedule for a seasonal launch looks like this.

| Milestone | What happens | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | Styles, colors, quantity, and delivery date shared with the concierge | Week 0 |
| Mockup and proof | Embroidery digitized, placement and thread colors approved | Confirmed with the quote |
| Production | Cutting, sewing, embroidery, quality review | 4–6 weeks after proof approval |
| Delivery | Individually wrapped, with custom gift packaging if specified | Transit quoted per destination |

Share the target date in the quote notes and the concierge builds the schedule backwards from it. Large amenity runs can be split into staged deliveries.

## Packaging and presentation

Totes ship individually wrapped. Custom gift packaging is available on every style and is where an amenity program earns its presentation: a tissue-lined box for a VIP arrival, a branded pouch for a retail sale, a printed card explaining the material. The Petite Mini Tote is itself the packaging in many arrival programs, holding smaller amenities from the accessories collection.

## Retail versus amenity: two briefs, one bag

A retail tote has to earn its price on a shelf next to fashion brands. That argues for the Atelier, the Sovereign, or the Envoy, a restrained logo, and packaging that supports the price point. An amenity tote has to survive sunscreen, sand, and a hundred laps of the pool deck, then still look good enough to pack. That argues for the Signature or the Nautical in a saturated color that hides wear. Properties running both tend to keep the colorway identical and let material signal the difference.

## Sustainability notes

Cotton canvas is a natural fiber that ages well and is easy to repair, which matters more to guests than any claim printed on a tag. Vegan leather avoids animal hide and gives consistent color across a run. For properties with stricter sourcing standards, the Bespoke Atelier can explore alternative fabrics and finishes; share the standard in the brief so it is evaluated against feasibility and quantity from the start.
