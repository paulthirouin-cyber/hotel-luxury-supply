# Resort Tote Styles Compared: Solid, Striped, Structured, Mini, and Drawstring

Material is one axis of a tote decision; silhouette is the other, and it is the one guests notice first. A squared tote and a drawstring bag in the same canvas and the same color serve entirely different moments. This comparison takes the eight silhouettes in the HL Supply collection and sorts them by shape, size, and the program each fits, ending with a decision table.

## Open totes: Signature, Nautical, Envoy

The open tote is the resort archetype: two handles, one compartment, nothing to fumble. The **Signature** is the pure version, a 16-by-14-inch cotton canvas in any color with the crest embroidered on the front panel; it is the amenity tote most properties start with. The **Nautical** is taller at 18 inches, with contrast stripes in a custom pairing that can match a cabana or an umbrella line, and it carries a rolled towel upright. The **Envoy** keeps the classic proportion and adds contrast accent straps in a second brand color, which makes it the open tote for a two-color palette and a sharper retail look.

## Structured: Pavilion

The **Pavilion** is the only squared silhouette. Its heavy-duty structured canvas holds an 18-by-12-inch box shape when empty, which means it stands on a shop counter, keeps purchases flat, and reads as modern rather than beachy. Properties with architectural interiors or an urban boutique tend to choose it; beach clubs rarely do.

## Leather and woven: Sovereign, Atelier

The **Sovereign** keeps a classic 15-by-16-inch canvas body and swaps the handles for genuine leather straps, which adds weight, patina, and a boutique price signal without a full leather bag. The **Atelier** is the statement: 20 inches wide, hand-woven vegan leather, high capacity, positioned as a fashion object on the boutique shelf and as a VIP gift. These two are the retail end of the collection; the [canvas vs woven comparison](guide:canvas-vs-woven-leather-tote) goes deeper on the material choice.

## Small: Petite

The **Petite** is 10 by 8 inches, a mini tote scaled for an arrival gift rather than a day out. It holds a bottle, a lens cloth, a sleep mask, and a card without looking empty, and it is the piece that turns a handful of accessories into a presentation. Properties also sell it as a charming boutique item for quick outings.

## Drawstring: Nomad

The **Nomad** is the only closed bag: a soft, breathable canvas that cinches with a durable drawstring. It is the wellness tote, carrying slippers, a yoga mat strap, and a bottle to the spa, and the gym bag for a fitness program. It is also the lightest to pack, which makes it a strong take-home piece for active properties.

## Building a family

A tote program rarely needs more than three silhouettes: an open amenity tote, a retail piece, and a small or specialized bag. The pairing that appears most often is Signature, Atelier, and Petite in one colorway with one crest, which covers the room, the shop, and the VIP arrival. Coastal properties swap the Signature for the Nautical; wellness properties add the Nomad; city hotels lead with the Sovereign or the Pavilion. Quote the family together so the combined quantity reaches a better tier and colors are matched on one proof.
