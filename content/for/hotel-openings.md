# Custom Guest Goods for Hotel Openings and Renovations

An opening is the one moment a property specifies everything at once, and the one moment the branded goods can be designed with the interiors rather than after them. The valet tray can match the headboard because the headboard fabric is on the same desk; the slipper can match the bath stone because the sample is in the same room. This page covers what new and renovated properties commission from HL Supply, how to brief it alongside the interiors, and how to schedule it to the handover.

## The opening collection

Most pre-opening programs come down to five groups.

1. **The suite set.** [The Concierge Valet Tray](product:concierge-valet-tray) on the nightstand, [The Hideaway Tissue Box Cover](product:hideaway-tissue-box-cover) in the bathroom and living area, [The Link Leather Cable Snap](product:link-cable-snap) on the desk, all debossed in one color matched to the interiors. [The Wayfarer Luggage Tag](product:wayfarer-luggage-tag) in the tray as the take-home piece.
2. **Turndown.** [The Cloud Fleece Slipper](product:cloud-fleece-slipper) or, for a contemporary scheme, [The Linear Ribbed Slipper](product:linear-ribbed-slipper), with [The Solace Sleeping Mask](product:solace-sleeping-mask) on the pillow.
3. **Hydration.** [The Aura Bottle](product:aura-bottle) in 350 ml beside a filtered-water station, replacing single-use plastic from day one.
4. **Arrival.** [The Signature Solid Tote](product:signature-tote) in the room, or [The Petite Mini Tote](product:petite-mini-tote) holding the mask, a lens cloth, and a welcome card.
5. **The boutique's first shelf.** [The Atelier Woven Leather Tote](product:atelier-woven-tote), [The Regent Classic Cap](product:regent-classic-cap), and the Aura in a retail size, in the signature color.

## Brief it with the interiors

The single most valuable thing an opening team can do is put the goods brief on the interior designer's desk. Share the FF&E palette, fabric and stone samples, and the brand guide at the same time; the concierge matches leather, textile, foam, and coating colors to those references and the proof confirms them on the actual materials. Logos applied tone-on-tone on leather and textiles let the room's palette lead. The [leather goods guide](guide:custom-hotel-leather-goods) explains how a suite set is specified from room types and key count; the [logo methods comparison](guide:embroidery-vs-deboss-vs-laser-etching) explains how one crest translates across deboss, embroidery, and etching.

## Quantities from the room count

An opening order is sized from keys, room types, and spares rather than guesses. Leather goods and hats start at 100 units with tiers at 100, 250, 500, and 1,000; bags, bottles, and flip-flops at 200 with tiers at 200, 500, 1,000, and 2,500; the sleep mask at 250; slippers at 1,000 pairs with tiers at 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, and 10,000. A 120-key property typically orders suite leather at the 100 or 250 tier per piece with 10 to 15 percent spares, slippers at the 2,500 tier with staged deliveries, bottles at 200 to 500, and the first boutique pieces at the lowest tier per style. Quote the collection as one project so tiers and colors are reviewed together; pricing is confirmed at quote review.

## The pre-opening timeline

Production runs 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, plus transit, and an opening has more pieces to proof than any other program. A workable schedule:

| Milestone | What happens | Timing before first guest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | Collection list, palette references, key count, brand guide, crest files | 14–16 weeks |
| Mockups | Each piece shown in the matched color with the logo at scale | 12–13 weeks |
| Proofs | Colors confirmed on actual materials; placements locked | 10–11 weeks |
| Production | All pieces produced and inspected | 4–6 weeks after proof approval |
| Staged delivery | Suite sets and turndown stock to the handover; slippers in stages; boutique pieces before soft opening | 2–3 weeks |

Share the opening date and the handover date in the quote notes. Soft openings often need only the suite set, slippers, and bottles; the boutique collection can follow by the public opening.

## Renovations

A renovation is an opening for part of the property, and the same logic applies: brief the goods with the new scheme, match colors to the new finishes, and replace the suite set, slippers, and bottles in the renovated rooms while leaving other floors on the existing program. The concierge can match a new color to an existing crest so the property reads as one identity across old and new rooms.

## Presentation on opening day

Suite pieces are placed, not packaged; the room is the presentation. Arrival gifts take custom packaging: the Petite with a card, or the Signature tote folded on the bed. The boutique's first pieces take hangtags and pouches. A short printed card in the room naming the materials (debossed leather, terry cotton, 304 stainless steel) tells the opening story quietly.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far before opening should guest goods be ordered?

Brief 14 to 16 weeks before the first guest. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, and an opening has many pieces to mock up and proof first.

### What should a new hotel order first?

The suite leather set, slippers, and a bottle for turndown; an arrival tote or mini tote; then the boutique's first pieces. Quote the collection as one project.

### Can the pieces match the new interiors?

Yes. Share the FF&E palette and material samples with the brief; every piece is produced in any custom color, matched to those references and confirmed on the proof.

### How are quantities sized for an opening?

From keys, room types, and spares: leather and hats from 100 units, bags and bottles from 200, the sleep mask from 250, slippers from 1,000 pairs, with staged deliveries for slippers.

### Does the same approach work for a renovation?

Yes. Brief the goods with the new scheme, match colors to the new finishes, and replace the suite set, slippers, and bottles on the renovated floors while keeping the crest consistent property-wide.
