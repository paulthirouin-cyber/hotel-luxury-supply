# Custom Golf Tournament Gifts and Pro Shop Goods for Clubs and Resorts

A tournament gift has a short life as a gift and a long life as a cap. Whatever goes into the welcome bag will be worn on the course for years, photographed at the 18th, and carried through airports, so it should look like the club rather than like a sponsor. This page walks through what golf properties commission from HL Supply, how the kit is assembled, and the quantities and timelines that make a tournament date hold.

The classic kit is a cap, a tote to carry it in, a bottle for the course, and one leather piece that outlasts the event. From the catalog:

## Event colorway, club identity

The strongest tournament programs use the club's standard embroidery and a dedicated event colorway rather than a different logo. The crest stays the same on every piece; the cap and tote arrive in a color chosen for the year, and a side embroidery carries the event name and date. Guests recognize the club at a glance, and the pro shop can carry the event color as a limited run. Every style in the catalog is produced in any custom color, with two-tone combinations available on caps, so matching a club tartan, a course signature color, or a sponsor-free palette is straightforward.

## Embroidery on the course

All hats and bags are embroidered in-house. For tournament caps, three decisions matter: the crest on the front panel in vector form, the event mark on the side (name and year read cleanly; long sponsor lists do not), and thread colors chosen against the cap color. Structured caps present the crest crisply, which is why the Fairway and the Regent are the tournament standards. The [hats buying guide](guide:custom-hotel-hats) and the [logo methods comparison](guide:embroidery-vs-deboss-vs-laser-etching) go deeper on digitizing and placement.

## The pro shop beyond the event

A tournament run is often the pro shop's first custom program, and the same proof can serve the shelf afterwards. A three-silhouette hat family (Fairway, Regent, and a seasonal Aero or Summit) in two colorways, a Signature tote, a Vanguard or Lumière bottle, and a Wayfarer tag make a coherent collection. Keep the embroidery consistent across pieces and let the event colorway become a limited edition. The [tote styles comparison](guide:hotel-tote-styles-compared) helps choose the bag.

## Quantities and tiers

Tournament fields are typically 100 to 250 players, which sits exactly on the catalog's lowest minimums: hats and leather goods start at 100 units with tiers at 100, 250, 500, and 1,000; bags and bottles start at 200 with tiers at 200, 500, 1,000, and 2,500. A 144-player member-guest usually orders caps and tags at the 100 tier and totes and bottles at the 200 tier with spares for the pro shop. The unit price follows the highest tier reached, so a club running two events a season often combines runs into one order at the next break and keeps stock in the shop. Pricing is confirmed at quote review once artwork and colors are set.

## Timeline from brief to first tee

Production runs 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, plus transit. Working backwards from a tournament date:

| Milestone | What happens | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | Kit contents, colorway, quantities, crest files, event mark | 10–12 weeks out |
| Mockup and proof | Embroidery digitized, placements and threads approved | Confirmed with the quote |
| Production | Caps, totes, bottles, leather pieces made and inspected | 4–6 weeks after proof approval |
| Delivery | Individually wrapped, kits assembled at the club or by request | 1–2 weeks before the event |

Share the tournament date in the quote notes and the concierge plans the schedule to it. For a late brief, the Signature tote and the Fairway cap are the fastest kit to proof because both use the club's existing crest.

## Presentation

Kits are assembled from individually wrapped pieces; custom gift packaging is available for the keepsake piece, and a printed card in the tote explaining the materials (performance twill, vacuum-insulated steel, debossed leather) is the detail that makes a kit feel considered. For a charity event, the card can carry the cause; for a member-guest, the pairings.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum order for golf tournament gifts?

Hats and leather goods start at 100 units, bags and bottles at 200. A 100-to-250-player field fits the lowest tiers; combining two events or adding pro shop stock often reaches the next price break.

### How far ahead should a tournament kit be ordered?

Brief 10 to 12 weeks before the event. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, and artwork and proofing come before that.

### Which cap is best for a tournament?

The Fairway Golf Cap: performance twill, structured front panels for a crisp crest, and a stay-dry headband. The Regent Classic Cap is the dressier alternative.

### Can the event name and date be added?

Yes. Side or back embroidery carries the event name and year alongside the club crest on the front; placements and threads are confirmed on the proof.

### Can we match the club's colors?

Yes. Every piece is produced in any custom color, including two-tone caps. Share Pantone references with the brief.
