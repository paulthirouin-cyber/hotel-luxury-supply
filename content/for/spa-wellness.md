# Custom Spa and Wellness Amenities for Hotels and Resorts

A spa is the one place on a property where the guest is paying attention to texture, weight, and quiet. The slipper's hand, the mask's strap, the bottle on the treatment ledge: every object is noticed, and every one is an opportunity for the property's identity to be present without being loud. This page covers what spa and wellness directors commission from HL Supply, how the pieces fit the spa journey, and the quantities and timelines that make a program run.

## One palette, tone-on-tone

Wellness collections work best in a single calm color drawn from the spa's interiors, a sand, a soft green, a stone grey, with logos applied tone-on-tone: thread one shade off the slipper and mask, an etch that reads as metal on the bottle, a quiet mark on the mat. Every piece in the catalog is produced in any custom color, so the slipper, mask, mat, and bag can be matched on one proof. ## Slippers: the program's center

Slippers are the wellness amenity used most and remembered longest. Two operating models decide the specification. A **replace-per-stay** program issues a fresh pair and lets the guest keep it, which favors the lighter Lattice or the eco Verdant and sizes the order to treatments and occupancy. A **launder-and-reissue** program needs the machine-washable Cloud with its anti-slip sole and sizes the order to the laundry cycle. Slippers start at 1,000 pairs with tiers at 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, and 10,000, and staged deliveries spread stock across a season. The [slippers buying guide](guide:custom-hotel-slippers) sizes a first order from key count, occupancy, and average stay.

## Hydration and the studio

A bottle in the treatment suite is usually 350 ml, slim and carafe-like; a bottle for the studio or a guided morning session is 500 ml. The Zenith yoga mat starts at 100 units, the lowest minimum in the catalog, which makes it easy to specify for a single studio or a retreat, and its extra-thick cushioning suits terraces and decks.

## Presentation

Slippers and masks ship individually wrapped, which is the standard treatment-suite presentation. For retreats and VIP wellness packages, custom gift packaging turns the mask, lens cloth, and a mini tote into a welcome set; the Vanity box is itself a presentation piece on the vanity. A printed card naming the materials (waffle-weave cotton, biodegradable fibers, 304 stainless steel) is the detail wellness guests read.
