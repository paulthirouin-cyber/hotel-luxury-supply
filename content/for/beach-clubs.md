# Custom Beach Club Merchandise and Pool Deck Amenities

A beach club is the most photographed part of a property, and almost everything in the frame can carry its identity: the stripe on the tote, the hat on the lounger, the flip-flops by the pool, the sunglasses on the bar. This page covers what beach clubs and pool decks commission from HL Supply, how to split issue from retail, and how to match a collection to the cabana stripes.

## Issue versus sell

Beach club programs run on two tracks. **Issued** pieces come with a cabana or a day pass: a tote at the desk, flip-flops in the locker, a bottle on the lounger. They need to be durable, easy to restock, and recognizable from across the deck. **Sold** pieces sit in the club shop: the bucket hat, the sunglasses, the striped tote, the sand-print flip-flop, the pieces guests want to own. The strongest clubs run both in one colorway so the guest sees a single identity whether the item was issued or bought.

## Matching the stripes

A beach club already has a palette: the umbrella canvas, the cabana curtain, the pool towel. The collection should borrow it rather than introduce a new one. Every piece in the catalog is produced in any custom color, and the Nautical accepts a custom color pairing for body and stripe, so the tote can repeat the umbrella stripe exactly. Share Pantone references or a swatch of the cabana fabric with the brief; the proof confirms the match on canvas, twill, foam, and acetate, which take color differently. The [tote styles comparison](guide:hotel-tote-styles-compared) and the [flip-flops guide](guide:custom-hotel-flip-flops) go deeper on each.

## The sand print

The Impression deserves its own paragraph because it is the piece that travels furthest. A reversed logo is molded into the outsole so every step leaves the club's mark in the sand, which guests photograph and post. Because a custom mold is tooled for the artwork, it needs the earliest brief in the program and a logo that reads when simplified to outsole scale. Clubs usually sell it rather than issue it, in the club's signature color, beside the Riviera bucket hat.

## Logo methods on the deck

Totes and hats are embroidered; flip-flops carry printed footbeds, strap logos, all-over prints, or the molded sand print; sunglasses and the umbrella handle are laser-etched; the bottle is etched. Supply vector artwork once and the concierge prepares the version each method needs; the [logo methods comparison](guide:embroidery-vs-deboss-vs-laser-etching) explains the differences. On the deck, a slightly larger, higher-contrast mark than a suite would use is usually right: the pieces are seen from a distance, in sun.

Production runs 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, plus transit. For a season opening, brief the Impression first (tooling), then the rest of the collection, and share the opening date in the quote notes so the concierge schedules deliveries before the first cabana is booked. ## Presentation

Issued pieces ship individually wrapped; a simple branded paper band suits a locker pair of flip-flops better than a box. Retail pieces take custom gift packaging: a pouch for sunglasses (the Pilot includes a carry case), a hangtag on the tote, a card explaining the sand print. Cabana welcome sets, a Signature tote holding an Essential pair, a Marquis bottle, and a lens cloth, are assembled from individually wrapped pieces.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should a beach club issue versus sell?

Issue durable, restockable pieces (a solid tote, Essential flip-flops, a bottle); sell the pieces guests want to own (the striped tote, the bucket hat, sunglasses, the sand-print flip-flop). Keep both in one colorway.

### Can the tote match our cabana stripes?

Yes. The Nautical Striped Tote is produced in any custom color pairing for body and stripe. Share Pantone references or a fabric swatch; the proof confirms the match.

### What is the sand-print flip-flop?

The Impression Flip Flop has a custom-molded outsole with a reversed logo that stamps the club's mark into the sand with every step. It needs the earliest brief because a mold is tooled for the artwork.

### What are the minimum orders?

Flip-flops, bags, and bottles from 200 units; hats from 100; sunglasses from 250; the umbrella from 200. Each has four quantity tiers confirmed at quote review.

### How long before the season should we order?

Brief 10 to 12 weeks before opening. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, and the Impression's tooling adds time, so brief it first.
