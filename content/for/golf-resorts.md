# Custom Pro Shop Goods and Member Gifts for Golf Resorts and Clubs

A golf property has three places where branded goods do real work: the pro shop, where the cap is the best-selling item on the floor; the member program, where a gift at renewal or a welcome at joining carries the club's standing; and the course itself, where the cart bottle, the locker-room amenity, and the umbrella at the first tee are the quiet signs of a property that has thought about its guests. This page covers what golf resorts and private clubs commission from HL Supply for each of the three, how to run them as one collection, and the quantities and timelines that fit a golf season.

## Three surfaces, one collection

The mistake most properties make is to brief the three surfaces separately: a sponsor-style cap for an event, a different cap for the shop, and a generic bottle on the cart. The stronger approach is one collection, one crest, one embroidery standard, and a seasonal colorway that moves across all three.

- **Pro shop retail.** A hat family of two or three silhouettes in two colorways, a tote, a bottle, and a leather piece: the pieces a member buys for a guest and a guest buys to remember the round.
- **Member gifts.** The same pieces in a presentation box: the renewal gift, the new-member welcome, the owners' weekend keepsake.
- **The amenity program.** The bottle on the cart, the umbrella at the tee, the lens cloth in the locker, the valet tray in the clubhouse suite: issued, not sold, and restocked each season.

## Seasonal colorway, permanent crest

The strongest club programs keep the crest and the embroidery standard fixed and change only the color each season. The cap and tote arrive in the year's colorway; the pro shop sells the previous season's as a limited run; the member gift carries the current one. Every style in the catalog is produced in any custom color, with two-tone combinations on caps, so matching a club tartan, a course signature color, or a quiet heritage palette is straightforward. Where an event at the property needs its own edition, a side embroidery with the event name and date is added to the standard cap rather than a new design being briefed.

## Embroidery for the crest

All hats and bags are embroidered in-house. For a club crest, three decisions matter: the crest on the front panel in vector form, any secondary mark on the side (club name, founding year, or an event line), and thread colors chosen against the cap color. Structured caps present the crest crisply, which is why the Fairway and the Regent are the club standards. ## Timeline for a golf season

Production runs 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, plus transit. Working backwards from opening day or the member renewal date:

| Milestone | What happens | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | Shop range, member gift, amenity list, colorway, crest files | 10–12 weeks out |
| Mockup and proof | Embroidery digitized, placements and threads approved | Confirmed with the quote |
| Production | Caps, totes, bottles, leather pieces made and inspected | 4–6 weeks after proof approval |
| Delivery | Individually wrapped; member gifts boxed on request; cart and locker stock staged | 1–2 weeks before the season |

Share the opening date or renewal date in the quote notes and the concierge plans the schedule to it. For a late brief, the Signature tote and the Fairway cap are the fastest to proof because both use the club's existing crest.

## Presentation

Shop pieces arrive individually wrapped with a hangtag on request. Member gifts take custom gift packaging: a box for the tag or the tray, a card carrying the member's name or the season. A printed card explaining the materials (performance twill, vacuum-insulated steel, debossed leather) is the detail that turns a gift into a keepsake. Amenity pieces ship without retail packaging so the locker room and the cart fleet restock quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum order for golf club merchandise?

Hats and leather goods start at 100 units, bags and bottles at 200. A club's shop order, member gift, and amenity restock combined usually reach the next price break.

### How far ahead should a season's collection be ordered?

Brief 10 to 12 weeks before opening day or the member renewal date. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, and artwork and proofing come before that.

### Which cap sells best in a pro shop?

The Fairway Golf Cap: performance twill, structured front panels for a crisp crest, and a stay-dry headband. The Regent Classic Cap is the dressier alternative and the usual member-gift cap.

### Can an event at the club have its own edition?

Yes. Side or back embroidery carries the event name and year alongside the club crest on the front, on the standard cap; placements and threads are confirmed on the proof.

### Can we match the club's colors?

Yes. Every piece is produced in any custom color, including two-tone caps. Share Pantone references with the brief.
