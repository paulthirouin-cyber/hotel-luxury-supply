# Custom Welcome Kits for Corporate Retreats and Destination Events

The best event gift is the one that does not look like an event gift. A retreat kit that reads as the property's own collection, with the group's mark applied quietly, is kept and used; a bag of sponsor logos is left in the room. This page covers what event planners, meeting teams, and properties hosting retreats commission from HL Supply, how co-branding works, and the quantities and timelines that fit an event calendar.

## The retreat kit

A kit that works on arrival, through the program, and on the flight home usually contains five things.

- **The carrier:** [The Pavilion Squared Tote](product:pavilion-squared-tote) stands on a registration table and keeps the kit presentable; [The Signature Solid Tote](product:signature-tote) is the softer alternative and the better beach companion for an incentive trip.
- **Hydration:** [The Lumière Bottle](product:lumiere-bottle), slim with an accent wrist strap, is the executive travel bottle; [The Aura Bottle](product:aura-bottle) in 500 ml sits on a boardroom table through a session.
- **Headwear:** [The Regent Classic Cap](product:regent-classic-cap) for a polished group cap; [The Heritage Dad Hat](product:heritage-dad-hat) for a relaxed incentive trip.
- **The flight home:** [The Solace Sleeping Mask](product:solace-sleeping-mask) and [The Clarity Lens Cloth](product:clarity-lens-cloth) in a [Petite Mini Tote](product:petite-mini-tote), the pieces that get used on the plane.
- **The keepsake:** [The Link Leather Cable Snap](product:link-cable-snap) for the desk after the retreat, or [The Wayfarer Luggage Tag](product:wayfarer-luggage-tag) for the traveler. For wellness-led retreats, [The Zenith Yoga Mat](product:zenith-yoga-mat) at a 100-unit minimum is a realistic group gift.

## Co-branding without clutter

Two marks on one object need a hierarchy. The pattern that works: the property's crest in its standard position (front panel of the cap, front of the tote, etched on the bottle) and the group's mark small and secondary, on the side of the cap, inside the tote, or as a second line beneath the etch. Event name and dates belong on a printed card in the kit, not on the goods. Every piece in the catalog is produced in any custom color, so a group color can be carried by the kit while the logos stay restrained; the [logo methods comparison](guide:embroidery-vs-deboss-vs-laser-etching) explains how embroidery, deboss, and etching each handle a second mark. Properties hosting many retreats often keep their standard collection and add only a card and a color for each group.

## Destination weddings and private events

The same kits serve destination weddings, milestone celebrations, and family gatherings, with the property's crest and the couple's or family's monogram as the second mark. The Riviera-style pieces, a Signature tote, a Heritage cap, a Marquis bottle, suit a beach wedding; the Pavilion, the Lumière, and the Regent suit a city celebration. Sleep masks and lens cloths in a Petite are the welcome-amenity classic for guests arriving from long flights.

## Quantities and tiers

Retreat and event groups typically run 50 to 300 people, which sits at or just above the catalog's lowest minimums: hats and leather goods start at 100 units with tiers at 100, 250, 500, and 1,000; bags and bottles at 200 with tiers at 200, 500, 1,000, and 2,500; the sleep mask and lens cloth at 250. For groups below a minimum, the property's standard collection is the answer: order the pieces at the minimum, use what the event needs, and keep the rest for the boutique or the next group. Properties hosting several events a season often order at the next tier and personalize each kit with a card. Pricing is confirmed at quote review once artwork and colors are set.

## Timeline for an event date

Production runs 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval, plus transit. Working backwards from the event:

| Milestone | What happens | Timing before the event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | Kit contents, group size, colorway, both logos in vector form, delivery address | 10–12 weeks |
| Mockup and proof | Logo hierarchy, placements, and threads approved | Confirmed with the quote |
| Production | Pieces made and inspected | 4–6 weeks after proof approval |
| Delivery and assembly | Individually wrapped pieces delivered to the property; kits assembled on site or by request | 1–2 weeks |

For a short lead time, pieces that use the property's existing crest (no new digitizing) proof fastest; a Signature tote and a Lumière bottle in the group color can be the fastest kit in the catalog.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum order for retreat welcome kits?

Hats and leather goods from 100 units, bags and bottles from 200, the sleep mask and lens cloth from 250. Groups below a minimum order the property's standard collection and keep the remainder.

### Can the kit carry both the property's and the group's logo?

Yes. The property's crest takes the standard position and the group's mark sits small and secondary (side of the cap, inside the tote, a second etched line); event names and dates go on a printed card.

### How far ahead should an event kit be ordered?

Brief 10 to 12 weeks before the event. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval; pieces using the property's existing crest proof fastest.

### Which pieces do guests actually keep?

The sleep mask and lens cloth (used on the flight home), the bottle, the cap, and the luggage tag or cable snap. The tote carries them all and becomes a beach or shopping bag.

### Do the same kits work for destination weddings?

Yes. The property's crest and the couple's monogram follow the same hierarchy; beach weddings suit a Signature tote, a Heritage cap, and a Marquis bottle, city celebrations the Pavilion, the Lumière, and the Regent.
